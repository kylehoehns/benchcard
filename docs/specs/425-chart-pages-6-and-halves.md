# Chart pages for 6 players and for two halves

## Issue

#425 — add a 6-player rotation chart page and one two-halves chart page, both
generated by `scripts/charts.mjs`, linked and in the sitemap.

## Goal

A coach who searches "6 player basketball rotation chart" or plays a league
with two halves lands on a finished, printable chart for that game, built by the
real engine, and one tap from the app and from the other charts.

## What would settle it

1. `SIZES` in `scripts/charts.mjs` is `[6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12]`.
   `app/6-player-basketball-rotation-chart.html` is generated like the others
   (4 × 8, every 4, seed 11, the first 6 of `sampleRoster`). Its plan prints
   24–28 minutes each, 8 stints.
2. A two-halves page exists at `/basketball-rotation-chart-two-halves`
   (file `app/basketball-rotation-chart-two-halves.html`), generated by
   `scripts/charts.mjs`: 10 players (first 10 of `sampleRoster`), 2 × 20
   minutes, `everyN` 4, seed 11. Its plan is 10 stints, 20 minutes each. Card
   header reads `10-PLAYER ROTATION` / `2 HALVES · 20 MIN`; the period labels on
   the card are the engine's own (`H1`, `H2`).
3. The halves page is **not** in `SIZES`: the seven size pages keep their shared
   4 × 8 format and `4 QUARTERS · 8 MIN` header.
4. Halves page copy (exact):
   - title: `Basketball rotation chart for two halves | Benchcard`
   - h1: `Basketball rotation chart for two halves`
   - description (≤160 chars): `A printable substitution chart for two
     20-minute halves: 10 players, subs every 4 minutes, 20 minutes each. Free,
     no signup, works offline.` — the minutes phrase comes from `facts(plan)`,
     like the size pages.
   - lede: `A finished, printable substitution chart for 10 players over two
     twenty-minute halves, subbing every four minutes: 20 minutes each, as close
     to equal playing time as the arithmetic allows, worked out before tip-off.
     Paste your own roster into Benchcard and it prints yours.`
   - caption: same sentence shape as the size pages (`f.stints` stints).
   - trust line, CTAs and footer: the same as the size pages. The second CTA is
     `Try it with 10 sample players` → `./?try=10`.
   - JSON-LD: WebPage named as the h1, breadcrumb Benchcard > About >
     `Two-halves chart`.
5. Links — every new page is linked from each place the size pages are:
   - **size pages ↔ halves page**: each size page keeps its "A different number
     of players?" nav (now 6 sibling chips) and gains a section
     `<h2>Playing two halves?</h2>` with a `.sizes` nav labelled
     `Other game formats` holding one chip `Two 20-minute halves` →
     `./basketball-rotation-chart-two-halves`. The halves page's section is
     `<h2>A chart for your roster size</h2>` with a `.sizes` nav labelled
     `Rotation charts by roster size`, seven chips `6 players` … `12 players`.
   - **about.html** and **advanced.html**: the roster-size nav gains `6 players`
     first; directly after it a second `.sizes` nav labelled
     `Rotation charts by game format` holds one chip `Two halves` → the halves
     page.
   - **index.html** welcome "Charts by team size" row: a `6` digit first
     (`aria-label="6-player rotation chart"`). The halves page is not added to
     this row (see Out of scope).
   - **404.html**: `6-player rotation chart` first, and
     `Two-halves rotation chart` last.
   - **sitemap.xml**: both new URLs, lastmod 2026-11-01, priority 0.7, the
     6-player entry before the 7, the halves entry after the 12.
6. Both pages pass every check the other chart pages pass: `charts.mjs --check`,
   `test/charts.test.js`, `test/seo.test.js` (WORDS row for the halves page:
   `basketball rotation chart`, `two halves`, `substitution`, `printable`,
   `equal playing time`), link-graph, storage (theme script), one-name,
   dead-static, static-pages (og), trust-line, analytics, and the smoke
   `static` row, which loads both new pages at 390 and 320 and at the 32px root.
7. The edit guard (`.claude/hooks/guard-edit.sh`) denies a file-tool edit to the
   halves page as it does to the size pages; `test/hooks.test.js` has the case.
8. Look check, `scripts/look.mjs` at 320 and 390 wide, 16 and 32px text, full
   height: the 6-player page, the halves page, about's and advanced's chart
   section, and the welcome screen's chart row. No clipped or cut text; the
   welcome row's seven digits do not leave one orphan digit on a line.

## Surfaces

Change: `scripts/charts.mjs`, the generated pages (regenerated, never hand
edited), `app/about.html`, `app/advanced.html`, `app/index.html`,
`app/app.css` (welcome chart row only), `app/404.html`, `app/sitemap.xml`,
`scripts/smoke/static.mjs` and its registry row name, `.claude/hooks/guard-edit.sh`,
the tests above, and docs that count "six" chart pages (`AGENTS.md`,
`docs/operations.md`, `docs/architecture/first-run.md`, comments that say six).

Must not change: `app/engine.js`, `app/roster.js`, `app/onboarding.js`,
`app/sw.js` (chart pages are not precached today; keep it so).

## Constraints

- One source for the chart list. Tests and smoke get the page list from
  `scripts/charts.mjs` exports (e.g. `SIZES` plus a halves descriptor and one
  list of every generated chart file/slug) rather than a second hand-kept list.
  `scripts/smoke/static.mjs` currently hard-codes `[7..12]`; it should read the
  export.
- `slug` is the address, `file` is the file on disk — keep them apart for the
  halves page too.
- The card is a real `generatePlan` run; nothing is drawn by hand. The halves
  card's names are all in `WIDTH_AT_100` (it is the same first-10 cast as the
  10-player page).
- The lede hedge stays: "as close to equal playing time as the arithmetic
  allows", never a flat "equal minutes".
- The privacy line is the existing trust line, unchanged.
- American spelling.
- Generated pages are edited only by running `node scripts/charts.mjs`.
- `about.html`'s "Last updated" and the sitemap lastmods are
  2026-11-01 (the build date moved; every changed page is bumped); `npm run check:history` must pass.
- 48px touch targets on every new chip and digit.

## Design

Generalize the page renderer to take a chart descriptor (slug, file, roster
size, format, header text, copy) so a size page and the halves page are the
same template. `pages()` returns all eight. The size-page copy builders stay as
they are for `SIZES`; the halves page has its own copy entries. The welcome row
CSS moves from "six in a row, 3 + 3 when narrow" to seven digits: one row when
the container fits 7 × 48px, otherwise 4 + 3.

## Proof

- `node --test test/charts.test.js` and siblings (node seam, generator
  exports and page source): items 1–7. The source-reading tests here are
  existing guards extended, under `/new-guard`.
- smoke `static` row (`node scripts/smoke.mjs --no-tests --only "<static row>"`):
  item 6's browser half.
- `scripts/look.mjs` + `/browser-verify`: item 8.
- Preview: both URLs return 200 and render.

## Out of scope

- The halves page is not on the welcome screen's "Charts by team size" row: it
  is not a team size, and the row's label would read wrong. It is linked from
  every chart page, about, advanced and 404.
- `?try=` stays an integer roster size. The halves page's Try button opens the
  same 10-player sample in the app's default 4 × 8; the coach changes periods in
  game setup. Making `?try=` carry a format is a separate change.
- No halves page for other roster sizes.
