Benchcard · prototype · round 4

Try it with your thumb

Combination 1 in Graphite, with team color as an option. Every rotation here comes from the app's own engine.js, unchanged, so what you see when you change something is what the real solver does.

Things to try

  1. Tap Panthers, then 9 players, and sit Harper out. The rotation rebuilds behind the sheet.
  2. Tap every 4 min and pick 3 min. Tap 4 × 8 and make it 2 × 16.
  3. Tap 2 rules. Remove one, then add "Never together".
  4. Switch Timeline to Card, then Shuffle.
  5. Start game. Step through the stints. Tap a player on the floor, then one on the bench.
  6. Go back to Today and open Hawks. It evens out the 9:00 game. Try turning that off.
  7. Add a game and try Use it on the first step.
  8. Tap Sample team at the top and switch to Wildcats. Then open Team › Settings › Team color.

Not in this prototype

  • By hand and Platoon strategies, lineup balance, and the starting-five, last-period and rest-limit rules. The full build keeps them, in the Plan sheet.
  • Printing, Share image, backup, first run, and the desktop layout.
  • Season numbers are examples. Nothing is saved: reload and it starts over.
