What's new

The latest changes, newest first.

Week of 2 November 2026

  • Everyone plays at least can now count whole periods: set it to 2 and each kid plays two full periods, start to finish.
  • A new setting, Everyone plays in, gets every kid on the floor at least once each half, or each period.
  • Plays at most N in a row now shows how many minutes in a row that is, goes as high as the game allows, and says that a break between periods does not reset the count.

Week of 26 October 2026

  • A re-plan during a game no longer rewrites stints already played, even under By hand or when a kid’s cap is already used up, and the plan warns you when a kid would sit out the whole second half.
  • A re-plan now counts the stints a kid has already played toward Plays at most, and once the last period is under way it leaves Starts the last period alone.
  • If Strongest five or Keep this five sends out an overtime five that breaks one of your rules, the plan now says so.
  • Everyone plays at least can be a share of the game instead of minutes, and a kid who arrives late is owed the same share of the time that is left.
  • Who’s here now marks a missing kid Out or Running late, and bench mode lists only the kids you’re still expecting. Anyone else who turns up is under Someone showed up?
  • Long names shrink to fit, then break with a hyphen, instead of splitting mid-word or getting cut off.
  • The team name at the top of Today is now a plain large title. Tap it to switch teams, as before.
  • In the iPhone Home Screen app, Share to print hands the card to the share sheet, where you can print it.
  • A new rule, Sits at most N in a row, keeps a kid from waiting on the bench too long, and the Rest limit is now called Plays at most N in a row.
  • Plans spread bench time out more, so a kid with a cap or in the starting five no longer sits out a whole half.
  • When two rules pull against each other, the notes under the plan now name the rule that won.
  • Bench mode keeps the Off and On call on screen at every stint, even on a small phone.
