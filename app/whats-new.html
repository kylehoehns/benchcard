What's new

The latest changes, newest first.

Week of 2 November 2026

  • Add a rule now sorts the kinds under three headings: one player, two players, and who starts and finishes. The kind you pick says what it does in one line.
  • Each Together rule now has its own choice: keep the two on together as much as possible, or always. It replaces the one switch that covered every pair in the game.
  • New in Settings: League rules. Everyone plays at least, Everyone plays in, and how long a player can stay on the floor or on the bench in a row are now in one place.
  • Each new game starts with your team's limits on playing or sitting in a row. To change one for a single game, tap it in that game's rules. Your team's limit stays the same.
  • Under Everyone plays at least, a line now says what your minimum comes to in a game, like 16 min in a 4 × 8 game.
  • Your league minimum can now be counted in quarters of time or in whole quarters, or halves if your games have two halves.
  • Plays at most and Sits at most are no longer in Add a rule. Turn them on in Settings under League rules.
  • Add a game now asks two things: who you're playing and who's here. A new game plans the same way as your last one. To change it, tap it in the sentence on the game screen.
  • On the plan sheet, one row, Make up minutes from, now replaces the two Even out switches. Pick This game only, Earlier today or The season so far.
  • Settings no longer asks how a new game starts. A new game keeps The season so far if you picked it last time.
  • On By hand, the plan no longer says it evens out the day, because your own minutes are the ones it uses.
  • In Settings, Best players under Fine-tuning now shows only once your players have different levels. Until then, a line says to set levels on the Team page.
  • A player's Level note now lists everything levels do. Under Order's two choices, a line says where to change the first one.
  • In the plan, switches a strategy doesn't use are now out of the way. A line under the strategy says which team settings it skips.
  • When you pick Platoon or Closers, the players now come filled in for you. Tap anyone to change them.
