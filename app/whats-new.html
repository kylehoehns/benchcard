What's new

The latest changes, newest first.

Week of 2 November 2026

  • If a kid shows up late, the time they sit on the bench now counts from when they arrived, not from the start of the game.
  • Overtime no longer counts toward your limit on how long a kid can sit in a row (Sits at most). A kid who sits at the end of the game and again in overtime is not warned about.
  • If a kid has a limit on how many minutes they can play and it covers the rest of the quarter or half, changing the plan partway through no longer takes them off before it ends.
  • After you swap a kid in or out, the plan now warns you if someone sits on the bench more in a row than your Sits at most limit allows.
  • When your league minimum is in full periods and you change the plan partway through a quarter or half, a kid who has played all of it so far stays on to finish it, and it counts as one of their full periods.
  • Sitting a kid for the rest of the game, or swapping one in for now, no longer fails when a Plays at most limit is in the way. The plan tells you who ends up over it.
  • Help, the tour and the About page now use plainer words and shorter sentences.
  • If Benchcard doesn’t load on a weak connection, it now tries once more by itself. If that fails too, it offers to save your backup and shows a Reload button instead of a blank screen.
  • New in Settings: you can set the minimum playing time in whole periods. Pick 2, and every kid plays two full periods, start to finish.
  • New in Settings: make sure every kid gets in at least once each half, or once each quarter.
  • The limit on how long a kid can play without a rest now shows the time in minutes, so you don’t have to do the math. It also goes higher than before. A break between periods doesn’t count as rest.

Week of 26 October 2026

  • If you change the plan during a game, the part already played stays as it was. Only the rest of the game changes.
  • The plan now warns you if a kid would sit out the whole second half.
  • When you change the plan mid-game, Benchcard remembers how long each kid has already been on the floor without a rest.
  • Changing the plan during the last period no longer swaps out the five you picked to start it.
